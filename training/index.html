Practice before your first visit

Ask a friend to play a busy restaurant owner. Introduce yourself in 20 seconds, ask one question, explain one relevant benefit, and show the correct QR. Practice ending politely when they say no.

“Hi, I’m an authorized What’s Out agent. Is this a good time for a quick question? How do you currently let nearby people know about your events? I can show you how What’s Out’s business and event tools work, if that would be useful.”

Use “authorized agent” only after your invitation has been accepted and your access is active. Never claim to be the founder or owner.

Check your understanding

Before you step out

  • Your invited email is verified and sales access is active.
  • Your own Business Owner QR opens and is readable on your phone.
  • You can explain the difference between a scan, a saved referral, and an eligible sale.
  • You will show current pricing in the app, not quote an unverified price.
  • You have a charged phone, internet access, and permission before collecting contact details.

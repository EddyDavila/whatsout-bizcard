My Sales

Follow referrals, verified business purchases, and commission review from the same records used by What’s Out.

Read-only dashboard. A saved referral is not a sale. Purchase verification, review, and external commission payment remain controlled by the existing backend and administrator.

Records are loaded in batches, then sorted by submission date. Counts describe loaded records only—not lifetime totals. Updates appear while connected; this does not certify a new purchase or issue a payment.
